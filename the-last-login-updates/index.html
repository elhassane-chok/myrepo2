1 · Legislation Watch — the NO FAKES Act

Not law as of August 22, 2026

Status: S.4591 / H.R.8915 — introduced May 2026, reported out of the Senate Judiciary Committee June 2026 (its furthest progress across four congressional attempts). Not passed by either chamber; nothing signed.

  • If enacted, Chapter 8's landscape changes overnight: a national licensable right against unauthorized digital replicas of voice and likeness, surviving death.
  • The state patchwork described in Chapter 8 (New York §50-f, Tennessee's ELVIS Act, California AB 1836/AB 2602) remains current law today.
